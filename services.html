FAQ

Common questions

Do I need to be home?

Not if there's street or side-gate access straight to the lawn — just leave a gate code or access notes on the quote form. If getting to the lawn means going through your garage or the house itself, you'll need to be home (or arrange access) so Eric can get through — let us know when you request your quote so it's not a surprise on the day.

What if it's raining on the day?

Light rain's fine. For genuinely wet ground or a storm, Eric will message you to reschedule — no fee for that.

How do I pay?

Bank transfer. A deposit (or 20% of the quoted price, whichever's higher) confirms your booking within 24 hours of accepting the quote; the rest is due on the day.

What if I'm not happy with the job?

Let us know within 24 hours of the mow and we'll make it right — usually by coming back to fix whatever's wrong. We don't offer refunds, but we're not leaving a job half-done either.

Can I cancel the monthly subscription?

Yes, anytime — no lock-in contract. Just let Eric know before the next scheduled visit.
