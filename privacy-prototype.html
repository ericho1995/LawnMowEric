Privacy Policy

Last updated: 6 September 2026

The Lawn Care (ABN 79 369 208 780), based in Melbourne, Victoria, respects your privacy. This policy explains what we collect through this website, why, and what you can do about it.

What we collect

When you submit a quote request, we collect:

  • Your name, phone number, and email address
  • Your suburb or postcode, and any address you enter to use the map tool
  • Lawn size (either selected or measured using the on-site mapping tool), how long since it was last mowed, and how often you want it mowed
  • Any notes you add — e.g. gate codes, pets, access instructions
  • The date and time you submitted the request

If you use the lawn-size mapping tool, your address search is sent to OpenStreetMap's Nominatim service and satellite map tiles are loaded from Esri, so those providers may log that request as they would for any map usage. We don't send them your name, phone, or email — only what you type into the address search box.

What we use it for

Solely to quote and book your lawn mowing job — nothing else. We don't sell, rent, or share your details with third parties, and we don't use them for marketing unless you separately ask to hear from us.

How it's stored

Quote requests are stored in a database only accessible to the site owner, gated by a private passphrase. This is a small operation — not enterprise-grade security — so please avoid including anything highly sensitive in the notes field.

How long we keep it

We don't currently have an automatic deletion schedule. If you'd like your information removed, contact us (below) and we'll delete it, unless we're required to keep records for tax or legal reasons (e.g. completed job invoices).

Payment information

We don't process card payments through this website. Bookings are confirmed with a deposit paid directly by bank transfer, using bank details we send you after quoting — we never ask for this through the website itself.

Your rights

You can ask us what information we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it, at any time.
