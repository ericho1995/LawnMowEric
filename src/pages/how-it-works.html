How it works

No phone call needed to get a price, and no app to download. Just a short form, a reply within one business day, and a day that suits you.

Four steps, start to finish

  1. Tell us about your lawn

    Your suburb, roughly how big the lawn is, and how often you want it mowed. Find your address on the map for your block size and an instant price range.

  2. Request a day

    Choose a day and a morning or afternoon, or tell us you're flexible. It's a request at this stage, not a booking.

  3. Get your quote

    Within one business day we confirm the price and your time, or offer the nearest free one. A deposit of $20 or 20%, whichever is higher, locks it in.

  4. We mow

    You get a message when we're on the way and another when we're done, with before-and-after photos. The rest is due on the day.

On the day

  • We use the gate code or access notes from your quote form, so you don't need to be home if there's side or street access.
  • Front and back are mowed, paths blown clear, and clippings mulched or put in your green bin.
  • Light rain is fine. For wet ground or a storm, we message you to reschedule, with no fee.
  • Not happy with something? Tell us within 24 hours and we'll come back and make it right.

Paying

Bank transfer. The deposit confirms your booking within 24 hours of accepting the quote, and the rest is due on the day. We never ask for card or bank details through this website.
